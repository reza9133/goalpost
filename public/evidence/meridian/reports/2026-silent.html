2026 climate report

Published April 2026

Emissions

We will cut our emissions intensity of Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions by 40% by 2035, measured against our 2022 baseline. Our emissions intensity fell 12% per megawatt-hour in 2025.

Scope

The target applies to plants in our continuing portfolio; assets held for sale are not included.

Assurance

Progress continues to be verified each year by an independent third-party assurance provider.

Operations

Two coal units at the Harrow site were retired in June 2025, and a 90 megawatt battery installation entered service in October.
