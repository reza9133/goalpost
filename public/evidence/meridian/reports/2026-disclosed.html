Restatement of our climate target

In 2026 the board revised the target we published in 2025. Our target is now a 40% reduction in emissions intensity per megawatt-hour by 2035, against a 2022 baseline. It replaces the absolute 2030 target, and we set out the reasons for the change in Section 4.

Scope

The revised target excludes assets held for sale. This is a change from the original pledge and is explained in Section 4.

Assurance

Progress continues to be verified each year by an independent third-party assurance provider.

Operations

Two coal units at the Harrow site were retired in June 2025, and a 90 megawatt battery installation entered service in October.
