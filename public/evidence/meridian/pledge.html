About this page

Meridian Grid Cooperative generates and distributes electricity for 410,000 member households. This page sets out the climate commitments our board adopted on 14 March 2025.

Emissions

We will cut our absolute Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions by 40% by 2030, measured against our 2019 baseline.

The target applies to every wholly owned power plant and excludes no facility.

Assurance

Progress will be verified each year by an independent third-party assurance provider.

Reporting

We publish an annual climate report each spring. Reports are listed under the reporting address registered for this pledge.
