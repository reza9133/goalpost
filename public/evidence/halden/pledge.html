Halden Institute for Cardiometabolic Research

Halden Cardiometabolic Trial: registered protocol

Version 1, registered 6 January 2025, before enrolment began

Purpose of this record

This page records the protocol for the Halden Cardiometabolic Trial exactly as it was registered on 6 January 2025, before the first participant was enrolled.

Primary outcome

The primary outcome is the change in seated systolic blood pressure from baseline to week 12.

Sample size

The trial will enrol 240 participants, randomised 1:1.

Analysis

The primary analysis will follow the intention-to-treat principle.
