Halden Cardiometabolic Trial: final report

Published 2 March 2026

Deviations from the registered protocol

We changed the primary outcome after registration. The primary outcome is now the change in 24-hour ambulatory diastolic blood pressure from baseline to week 24, replacing seated systolic blood pressure at week 12.

Recruitment stopped early because of funding limits, so the trial enrolled 186 participants instead of the planned 240.

The primary analysis followed the per-protocol principle, a departure from the registered plan.

Results

Mean diastolic blood pressure fell by 4.3 mmHg more in the treatment group than in the control group.
