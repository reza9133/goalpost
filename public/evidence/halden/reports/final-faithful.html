Halden Institute for Cardiometabolic Research

Halden Cardiometabolic Trial: final report

Published 2 March 2026

Methods

The primary outcome was the change in seated systolic blood pressure from baseline to week 12, as registered.

The trial enrolled 240 participants, randomised 1:1.

The primary analysis followed the intention-to-treat principle.

Results

Mean systolic blood pressure fell by 6.1 mmHg more in the treatment group than in the control group.
